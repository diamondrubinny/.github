# Code of Conduct

This community follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short
- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, regardless of background, language or experience level.
- Hebrew, English and other languages are all welcome — be patient with translation.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who repeatedly do so.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer via
[rubinny.co.il](https://rubinny.co.il). All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.
